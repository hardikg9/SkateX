SkateLog Privacy Policy

Version 1.0-draft · Effective [date]

1. Who we are

SkateLog is provided by [legal entity name], [registered address] ("we", "us"). We are the Data Fiduciary for the personal data described here under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the DPDP Rules, 2025, and the controller for users in other countries.

Contact and grievances: [Grievance Officer name], [email], [phone]. We respond to grievances within [the period required by law].

2. Who uses SkateLog

The account holder is a parent or legal guardian. Skaters are often under 18; a skater's profile is created and managed by their parent or guardian, and we process a child's data only with that person's verifiable consent.

3. What we collect, and why

DataWhy
Your mobile number (and email, if you add it)To sign you in, secure your account and contact you about it
Your name, your relationship to the skater, language and unitsTo set up the account and record your consent
Skater nickname, year of birth, skate type, level, club (optional)To show results by age group and level. We do not ask for full names, schools or photos
Sensor recordings (motion data), lap times, technique measuresTo produce the session results you and approved coaches see
Rink location (venue only) and phone placement at the rinkTo identify the rink and time laps. We never store a child's home or route
Taps and corrections you makeTo improve the results of that session
Basic app diagnostics (app version, phone model, errors)To fix problems

We do not track children across other apps or sites, profile them for advertising, or show them targeted ads.

4. Optional research sharing

If you turn it on, recordings are shared without names or contact details to improve lap and technique detection. It is off by default and you can turn it off at any time in Settings.

5. Who can see the data

  • You, and other guardians you add to the skater's profile.
  • Coaches or clubs only after you approve them; you can withdraw access at any time.
  • Service providers who host or process data for us under contract ([hosting provider, SMS provider]).
  • Authorities, only where the law requires it.

We do not sell personal data.

6. Where data is stored

[Data is stored in India / region], with backups in [region]. If data is transferred outside India, we do so only as the DPDP Act and Rules allow.

7. How long we keep it

While the account is active. If you delete the account, we delete personal data within [period], except where the law requires us to keep something longer. Accounts inactive for [period] are deleted after notice.

8. Your rights

  • See and download your and your skaters' data (Settings › Download my data).
  • Correct or complete it.
  • Withdraw consent and delete the account and all data (Settings › Delete account).
  • Raise a grievance with us, and then with the Data Protection Board of India.
  • Nominate another person to act for you if you die or are unable to.

Users in the EU/UK and other regions have the rights of their local law; contact us to use them.

9. Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, access is limited to staff who need it, and we will tell you and the authorities about a personal data breach as the law requires.

10. Changes

We will notify you in the app before material changes, and ask for consent again where needed.
